Sometimes you help an organization without a shift in the app, for example at an event the coordinator tracked on paper. You can submit those hours yourself, and the organization reviews them.

## Submit hours

1. Open **Impact** and choose to log hours.
2. Pick the **organization** you served.
3. Enter the **date** you served. It must be within the **last 12 months** and not in the future.
4. Enter the **time** in 15-minute steps, from 15 minutes up to 12 hours.
5. Describe what you did in 10 to 500 characters, for example "Sorted cans at the spring food drive".
6. Submit.

If the date is too old or in the future, you'll see "Pick a date in the last 12 months, not in the future."

## What happens next

- Your entry is **pending** until a coordinator at that organization reviews it. Pending hours don't count yet.
- When it is **approved**, the hours appear on Impact and can go on a verified letter if the organization is verified.
- If it is **rejected**, you get an alert with the coordinator's reason. Rejected hours never count.

Pressing submit twice by accident creates only one entry.

## Tips

- Be specific in the description so the coordinator recognizes the event.
- Hours from shifts you checked in to with the kiosk code are counted for you. Don't submit those again.
