You can add any shift you signed up for to the calendar app on your phone or computer.

## Add a shift

1. Open the shift where the button says **Signed up**, or find the shift in **My Shifts**.
2. Press **Add to calendar**. A small calendar file (.ics) downloads.
3. Open the file. Your calendar app (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, and others) asks to add the event.

The event has the shift name, organization, place, and time in the organization's time zone, so it shows at the right time wherever you are.

This is a one-time download, not a live feed. If the organization moves the shift, you get an alert in the app. Download the file again so your calendar has the new time.

## If a shift is cancelled

When you cancel, or the organization cancels the shift, the shift shows a **Download cancellation** link. Open that file to tell your calendar to remove the event.

Cancellation is best effort. Some calendar apps, including Google Calendar imports, may ignore it. **If your calendar still shows this shift, delete the event.**

## Reminders

The app shows reminders on **My Shifts** and **Explore** for shifts starting within 24 hours. These appear only inside the app. If you want a phone reminder, add the shift to your calendar and set one there.
