When every seat on a shift is taken, you can often join its **waitlist**. If someone cancels, the first person in line gets the seat.

## Join a waitlist

1. Open the shift. If it is full, the button says **Join waitlist (#N)**, where N is the place in line you would get.
2. Press it. The button changes to **Waitlisted #N of M**, which shows your place and how many people are waiting.

A waitlist holds as many people as the shift has seats. When it is also full, you'll see "This shift and its waitlist are full." Pick another date.

You can press **Leave waitlist** any time before the shift starts. Leaving a waitlist never counts against you.

## Getting promoted

When a confirmed volunteer cancels, the person who has waited longest moves up automatically. You get an alert and a banner on Explore, such as "You're in! Saturday 9 AM", with two choices:

- **Confirm**: keep the spot. Nothing else to do.
- **Can't make it**: give the spot back so the next person can have it.

A coordinator adding seats to a shift can also promote people from the waitlist.

## Releasing a promoted spot without penalty

If you were promoted and can't go, press **Can't make it** within **24 hours** of the promotion. It does not count as a late cancel. If the promotion happened less than 24 hours before the shift starts, you can release it without penalty any time before the start.

After that window, use **Cancel** instead. If you see "This spot can no longer be released without counting as a late cancel", the window has passed. A cancel less than 24 hours before the start is recorded as a late cancel.

If you were promoted less than 24 hours before the start and still miss the shift, it is marked **excused**, not a no-show.

## The waitlist closes 2 hours before the start

**2 hours before** a shift starts, its waitlist closes. Anyone still waiting is taken off the list and gets an alert. This never counts against you. After that, nobody is promoted, and a full shift says "This shift is full and its waitlist has closed."

If a seat opens up after the waitlist closes, anyone can take it as a walk-up until the shift starts, and the button shows **Sign up** again.
