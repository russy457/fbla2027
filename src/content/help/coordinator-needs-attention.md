**Needs attention** is a list on your organization's **Dashboard**. It collects everything that needs a person to decide, grouped by shift.

## What shows up

- **Hours to review**: volunteers who checked in but never checked out. Their time is counted up to the scheduled end and left pending.
- **Manual hours**: hours a volunteer submitted for service outside the app.
- **Review requests**: a volunteer asked you to look again at a no-show.

When the list is empty, it says "Nothing needs your review."

## Approve or reject hours

- Press **Approve** on a row to approve it.
- Press **Approve all** to approve every kiosk-verified row for one shift at once (up to 50).
- Press **Reject** to decline a row. A reason is required, and the volunteer sees it.

Approved hours count on Impact and on verified letters. Rejected hours never count. Once hours are approved, change them only through attendance tools on the shift roster.

## Handle a review request

Pressing a review request opens the attendance tools for that signup. Read the volunteer's note, then:

- Change the no-show to **completed** (enter minutes in 15-minute steps) or **excused**, or
- **Keep** it as a no-show.

Every choice needs a short note, closes the request, and alerts the volunteer.
