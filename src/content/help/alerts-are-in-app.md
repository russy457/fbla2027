The app keeps you up to date with **alerts inside the app only**. We do not send email, text messages (SMS), or push notifications, so check the app before a shift.

## Where to find alerts

- A **badge** in the header shows how many alerts you haven't read (up to **99+**).
- Press the badge to open your **Notifications** list. Alerts are grouped by day.
- Press an alert to go to the shift, your Impact page, or My Shifts.
- Press **Mark all read** to clear the badge.

If you have none, you'll see "No alerts yet. Alerts appear here, in the app only."

## What you get alerts about

- **Promoted from a waitlist**: "You're in!" with **Confirm** and **Can't make it**.
- **Waitlist closed** before you got a spot.
- **Shift cancelled** or **shift moved** by the organization.
- **Hours approved** or **not approved**, with the reason.
- **Attendance updated** by a coordinator.
- **Letter changed** or **letter revoked**.
- For coordinators: a volunteer **asked for a review** of a no-show.

## Reminders

Shifts starting within the next 24 hours show as reminders on **My Shifts** and **Explore**. Reminders are not added to the Notifications list.
