## Display settings

The **Display** controls are at the bottom of every page.

- **Text size**: choose 100%, 125%, or 150%. Every screen is built to work at 150%.
- **High contrast**: darker text, stronger borders, and a thicker focus outline.
- **Reduce motion**: turns off animations. The app also follows your device's reduce motion setting.

Changes apply right away and are remembered on this device.

## Status is never color alone

Every status, such as approved, pending, or revoked, shows an icon and a word, so you never need to tell colors apart.

## Keyboard

- Press **Tab** to move between controls. The first stop is **Skip to main content**.
- Press **?** on any page (when you are not typing in a box) to open quick help.
- Press **Esc** to close quick help. Focus goes back to where you were.
- Signing up, typing a check-in code, and running the kiosk all work with a keyboard.

Buttons and links are at least 44 pixels tall so they are easy to tap.
