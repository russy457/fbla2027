Any adult with a verified email can register a nonprofit and start posting shifts.

## Before you start

- You must be **18 or older**. Students can ask an adult leader to register, then join as a coordinator with an invite code.
- Your email must be **verified**. Use the link we emailed you.
- Have the nonprofit's **EIN** ready, written as **NN-NNNNNNN** (for example 12-3456789).

## Register

1. Open [Register an organization](/org/register).
2. Enter the **name** (2 to 80 characters) and **mission** (up to 500 characters).
3. Pick 1 to 3 **cause areas**.
4. Enter the **EIN**, **address**, and **contact email**. Phone and website are optional.
5. Check the **time zone**. Shift times show in this zone. It starts as Central Time.
6. Press **Register**.

You become the organization's **owner**, and your Dashboard says "Your organization is ready." Press **Create your first shift** to begin.

Pressing Register twice by accident still creates only one organization.

## Verification pending

Every new organization starts **unverified** until an admin reviews it. You can create shifts and run them right away, but until you are verified:

- Volunteers see an **Unverified** chip: "Hours here won't appear on verified letters until this organization is verified."
- Volunteers under 18 can't sign up for your shifts.
- You see only display names for volunteers under 18.

When an admin verifies you, these limits lift on their own. Changing the organization's name or EIN later in **Settings** sets it back to unverified.
