Milestones celebrate the time you give. There are three: **25**, **50**, and **100 approved hours**.

## Track your progress

Open **Impact**. Under your total approved hours, a progress bar shows how close you are to the next milestone. If you are just starting, it says "0 of 25 hours to your first milestone".

Right after you check out of a shift, your phone also shows how many hours were logged and your progress toward the next milestone.

## What counts

Only **approved** hours count, from kiosk check-outs, coordinator approvals, and approved manual hours. Pending and rejected hours don't count until they are approved.

## Earning a badge

When your approved hours reach 25, 50, or 100, you earn that milestone's **badge**. The app celebrates once, and the badge stays on your Impact page as a badge card.

If you turned on **Reduce motion**, the celebration is shown without animation.

## Badges and reports

Your **volunteer hours report** includes a milestones section, so you can share your badges along with your hours. For official proof of hours, use a verified letter.
