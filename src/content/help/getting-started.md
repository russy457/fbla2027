This app helps nonprofits run volunteer shifts and helps volunteers keep a record of the hours they serve. Many volunteers are high-school students who need proof of service hours, so every hour in the app comes from a real check-in or a coordinator's approval.

## The volunteer loop

1. **Find a shift** on Explore and open it to see the date, time, place, and seats left.
2. **Sign up** with one button. Your spot is saved when the button says Signed up.
3. **Check in** at the event by typing the 6-digit code shown on the organization's kiosk tablet.
4. **Check out** the same way before you leave. Your hours are counted for you.
5. **Get a verified letter** from Impact. Anyone can confirm it on the Verify page.

## Getting around

On a phone, the tabs at the bottom are Explore, My Shifts, Impact, and Help. On a larger screen the same four items sit in the top bar.

- **Explore** lists upcoming shifts.
- **My Shifts** shows your next shift and the Check in button.
- **Impact** shows your approved hours and your letters.
- **Help** is this Help Center. Press the **?** key on any page to open quick help.

You can browse Explore, read help articles, and check a letter on the Verify page without signing in.

## Roles

- **Volunteers** sign up for shifts, check in and out, and request letters.
- **Coordinators** run shifts for an organization: they start the kiosk, watch arrivals, and review hours.
- **Owners** registered the organization and can do everything a coordinator can.

If an action says "Finish setting up your profile first", complete your account setup and try again.
