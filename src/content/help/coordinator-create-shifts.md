Shifts live under an **opportunity**, which describes the work (for example "Food bank sorting"). Each opportunity can have many **shifts**, each with its own date, time, and number of seats.

## Use the shift planner

On **Shifts**, press **Create shift**. At the top is a text box. Type what you need in plain words, for example:

"need 12 people Sat 9-1 sorting at the food bank"

Press **Parse**. The planner fills in the form below: the number of people, the day, the start and end time, and place or cause words it recognizes. Filled fields are highlighted. Check each one and change anything it got wrong. Nothing is created until you press **Create shift**.

## Fill in the form

Opportunity details:

- **Title** (4 to 80 characters) and **description** (up to 2,000 characters).
- **Cause area**, **type** (one-time, recurring, virtual, or skilled), and up to 10 **skills**.
- **Minimum age**, from 13 to 21. It starts at 13.
- **Location**. Virtual opportunities have none.

Shift details:

- **Start** and **end** time, in your organization's time zone. The end must be after the start, the shift can be at most **12 hours** long, and it must be in the future.
- **Seats**, from 1 to 200.

## Edit a shift

Open the shift and change its time or seats before it starts.

- **Adding seats** promotes people from the waitlist right away, if the waitlist is still open.
- **Lowering seats** below the number of people already signed up is refused: "Remove volunteers first."
- **Moving the time** alerts everyone signed up so they can update their calendar.

## Cancel a shift

Open the shift and press **Cancel shift**, then give a short reason. Everyone signed up or waiting is alerted, and it never counts against them. If the shift is already under way, volunteers who checked in are marked completed with hours counted up to the cancel time, pending your review. A shift that has ended can't be cancelled.
