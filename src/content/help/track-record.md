Your **track record** shows how often you come to shifts you signed up for. You'll find it on **Impact**.

## What it shows

It is plain text, such as "Attended 8 of 10 recent shifts", never a colored grade. It looks at your **last 20 finished shifts** from the **past 12 months**.

These count:

- Shifts you **completed**.
- **No-shows**: you had a confirmed spot but never checked in.
- **Late cancels**: you cancelled less than 24 hours before the start. A late cancel weighs half as much as a no-show.

These never count against you:

- Cancelling 24 hours or more before the start.
- Leaving a waitlist, or being taken off one when it closes.
- Releasing a promoted spot within the allowed window.
- Shifts the organization cancelled.
- Shifts marked **excused**.

With fewer than 3 finished shifts, you are shown as a **New volunteer** instead.

## Who can see it

Only you, and coordinators of organizations where you have signed up. It never appears on public pages, in search, or on the kiosk, and it never stops you from signing up.

## Ask for a review of a no-show

If you were marked a no-show by mistake, for example you were there but forgot to check in:

1. Find the no-show under **Past shifts** on **My Shifts**.
2. Press **Request review**.
3. Explain what happened in 10 to 500 characters and press **Send request**.

You can ask within **30 days** of the shift. After that you'll see "Reviews can be requested within 30 days of the shift." Contact the organization instead. A coordinator looks at your request and either updates your attendance or keeps it, and you get an alert either way.
