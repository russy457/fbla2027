Mistakes happen. A volunteer might help all morning but forget to check in, and the shift marks them a **no-show**. A review request lets them say so, and a coordinator decides.

## For volunteers: request a review

1. Open **My Shifts** and find the past shift, or open **Your track record** on **Impact**.
2. Press **Request review** next to the no-show.
3. Explain what happened in 10 to 500 characters, for example "I was at the sorting table from 9 to 1 but forgot to check in."
4. Send it.

Things to know:

- You can request a review only for a **no-show**, within **30 days** of the shift.
- Sending it again while it is open changes nothing. Your first request is still waiting.
- The organization's coordinators get an alert. You get an alert when they decide.

## For coordinators: resolve a request

Open requests appear in **Needs attention** on your Dashboard. Open one, read the note, and choose:

- **Completed**: enter the minutes served, in 15-minute steps up to the scheduled length. The hours are approved.
- **Excused**: no hours, and it no longer counts as a no-show.
- **Keep**: leave it as a no-show.

A note is **required** every time. It is saved with the change, closes the request, and the volunteer is alerted.

## What changes after a decision

- A no-show changed to completed or excused stops counting against the volunteer's track record.
- Approved hours from a completed change can go on verified letters.
