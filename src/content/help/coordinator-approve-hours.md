## Approved automatically

When a volunteer checks in and out with the kiosk code, their hours are **approved right away**. The kiosk code is the proof, so you don't need to review those.

## Hours that need review

If a volunteer checked in but never checked out, the app counts their time up to the scheduled end when the shift finishes. Those hours are **pending** and marked for review, because nobody confirmed when they actually left.

## Reviewing hours

> Coming soon: the Needs attention list on your Dashboard. Until it arrives, pending hours stay pending.

When it arrives, the Dashboard will group items by shift:

- **Approve** one row, or **Approve all** for kiosk-verified rows of one shift (up to 50 at a time).
- **Reject** a row with a required reason. The volunteer sees the reason.
- Rejected hours never count toward letters.
- Once approved, hours change only through attendance tools.
