## Check out

Before you leave, open **My Shifts**, press **Check out**, and type the current kiosk code.

- Check-out opens **15 minutes after** you checked in.
- It closes **30 minutes after** the scheduled end. After that, your coordinator confirms your hours instead.

## How your hours are counted

The app counts only the time **inside the scheduled shift**. Arriving early or staying late doesn't add time, and the result is rounded to the **nearest 15 minutes** (exactly halfway rounds up).

For a shift scheduled 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM:

- In at 8:45, out at 1:10: **4 hours** (only 9:00 to 1:00 counts)
- In at 9:07, out at 12:52: **3 hours 45 minutes**
- In at 9:00, out at 9:20: **15 minutes**

If a coordinator needs to adjust your time, they do it through attendance tools.

## When hours are approved

Hours from a kiosk check-out are **approved right away** and show on Impact.

## If you forget to check out

About 30 minutes after the shift ends, the app finishes the shift. If you checked in but never checked out, your hours are counted up to the scheduled end and marked **pending** until a coordinator reviews them.
