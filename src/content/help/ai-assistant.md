## Ask a question

1. Open **Help**, or open quick help on any page.
2. Type your question in the **Ask** box, such as "when does check-in open".
3. Press **Ask**.

Questions can be up to **2,000 characters**. A counter appears when you get close. If a question is too long, you'll see "Keep it under 2,000 characters."

You need to be signed in with a finished profile to ask. When you are signed out, you still see matching help articles, plus a **Sign in to ask** button.

## How answers work

The app finds the help articles that best match your question. An AI model then writes a short answer using **only the text of those articles** and the name of the page you are on. Links to the articles it used appear under the answer, so you can read the full steps.

Every answer has a label:

- **AI answer, may be wrong**: written by the AI. Check the linked article before acting on it.
- **From Help Center**: the 3 best matching articles, with no AI.

You get **From Help Center** results instead of an AI answer when the AI is turned off or unavailable, when it takes longer than **10 seconds**, or when you have reached your limit.

## Limits

- **20 questions per hour** and **100 per day** for each person.
- There is also a daily limit for the whole site. When it is reached, everyone gets Help Center results until the next day.

When you hit your limit you'll see "You've reached today's assistant limit; here are matching help articles."

## Privacy

The assistant never sees your profile, your shifts, your hours, or anyone's contact details. It only reads help articles and the page name. Please **don't type private information**, such as phone numbers or addresses, into the Ask box.

Answers are plain text. The assistant can't take actions for you, such as signing you up or changing hours.
