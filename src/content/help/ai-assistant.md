## Ask the Help Center

Type a question in the **Ask a question** box, such as "when does check-in open". The answer is a short list of the help articles that match best, labeled **From Help Center**. Open an article to read the full steps.

Questions can be up to 2,000 characters. A counter appears when you get close.

## The AI assistant

> Coming soon: signed-in volunteers will be able to ask the AI assistant. Until then, every answer comes from Help Center articles.

When it arrives:

- AI answers will be labeled **AI answer, may be wrong**. Check the linked article before acting on it.
- The assistant will read only help article text and the name of the page you are on. It will never see your profile, your shifts, or anyone's contact details.
- There will be a daily limit. When you reach it, you'll get matching help articles instead.
- If you are signed out, you'll see article results and a prompt to sign in.
