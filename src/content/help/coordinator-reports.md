## Two reports

**Volunteer hours report** (for volunteers, from Impact): a summary, hours by organization, hours by month, your shift list, and milestones.

**Organization participation report** (for coordinators, from Reports): signups, attendance rate, total approved hours, hours by opportunity and by month, attendance by status, and top volunteers by display name.

Attendance rate is completed signups divided by completed plus no-shows.

## Building a report

1. Pick the report type and a date range.
2. Turn sections on or off.
3. Pick one of six themes, including a high-contrast theme.
4. Press **Generate PDF**. If it fails, press Retry.

You can also **Export CSV** of data you can already see, for use in a spreadsheet.
