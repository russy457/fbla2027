## "That code is wrong or expired"

Codes change every 30 seconds. Look at the kiosk and type the code that is showing **now**. Check that you typed all 6 digits.

## "Check-in for this shift is not open right now"

Check-in opens 30 minutes before the start time and closes at the scheduled end. The message tells you when it opens. If the shift already ended, ask the coordinator to record your attendance.

## "Too many attempts, wait a minute"

To stop guessing, you get **10 tries every 10 minutes**, shared between check-in and check-out. The message shows a countdown. Wait for it to finish, then type the current code carefully.

## "You don't have a confirmed spot on this shift"

You need to be signed up before you can check in. If seats remain, sign up first, then check in.

## "Check-out opens at..."

Check-out opens 15 minutes after you check in. Wait until the time shown.

## "Check-out for this shift has closed"

Check-out closes 30 minutes after the scheduled end. You don't need to do anything else: your coordinator will confirm your hours.

## "This shift was cancelled by the organization"

The organization cancelled the shift, so check-in is off. Pick another shift on Explore.

## For coordinators: "Kiosk session expired"

The kiosk tablet needs a coordinator to sign in again. See the kiosk article for details.
