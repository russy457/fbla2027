## Birth date comes first

Setup asks for your birth date **before** an account is created. You must be **13 or older** to use this app. If you are younger, setup stops with "You must be 13 or older to use this app" and no account is made. Ask a parent or guardian about volunteering together.

## Why we need your birth date

Your age decides which shifts are safe and open to you. The app checks your age at the moment it matters, not once at sign up:

- Some shifts have a minimum age, such as 16 and older.
- Volunteers under 18 can only join shifts from organizations that have been verified.
- Only adults (18 and older) can register an organization.

Your birth date stays private. Coordinators never see it, and it is not shown on any public page. It can't be changed from your profile; if it is wrong, an admin can correct it.

## The setup steps

1. Birth date
2. Email and password
3. Your first and last name (phone is optional)
4. Interests, so Explore can suggest causes you care about
5. Skills, availability, and ZIP code (you can skip these)

A progress indicator shows which step you are on. When you finish, you can start signing up for shifts.
