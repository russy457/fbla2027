## Finalizing a shift

A shift is **finalized** automatically about 30 minutes after it ends. You can also finalize it yourself from the shift once it has ended; before then you'll see "This shift hasn't ended yet." Finalizing twice is safe and changes nothing.

## What finalizing does

- **Checked in and out**: stays completed with approved hours.
- **Checked in but never checked out**: marked completed, with hours counted up to the scheduled end and left **pending** for your review.
- **Signed up but never checked in**: marked as a **no-show**.
- **Promoted from a waitlist less than 24 hours before the start**: marked **excused**, not a no-show.
- **Cancelled signups**: unchanged.

After finalizing, contact details for that shift are frozen and the shift is marked finalized.

## Correcting attendance

> Coming soon: attendance tools on the shift roster.

When they arrive, each change needs a short note and the volunteer is told about it:

- Change a no-show to **excused** (no hours) or to **completed** with minutes in 15-minute steps, up to the scheduled length.
- Change a completed signup to **no-show**, which removes its hours from letters.
- Volunteers can ask for a review of a no-show within 30 days of the shift.
