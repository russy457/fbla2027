Every organization on the app has its own public page. Anyone can open it, signed in or not.

## How to get there

Press an organization's name anywhere you see it: on a shift in **Explore**, in the **Recommended** cards, on a shift page under **Organization**, or on your **Saved** page.

## What the page shows

- The organization's **name** and a **Verified organization** chip, or an **Unverified** chip. Hours at unverified organizations don't appear on verified letters until the organization is verified, and volunteers under 18 can join their shifts only after that.
- Its **mission**, the causes it works on, and its address.
- **Upcoming shifts**, soonest first, with the date, time, and seats left. Press **Open next shift** to go straight to the next one.

If nothing is scheduled you'll see "No upcoming shifts right now."

## Save an organization

When you are signed in, press **Save** to keep the organization on your **Saved** page. Saving is private and does not sign you up for anything. Press **Saved** to remove it again.
