## Who can register

For the step-by-step form, see [Registering your nonprofit](/help/org-registration).

To register an organization you must:

- Be **18 or older**. If you are younger, ask an adult leader to register.
- Have a **verified email**. Use the link we emailed you.
- Enter the nonprofit's EIN as **NN-NNNNNNN**.

The person who registers becomes the organization's **owner**.

## What "Unverified" means

Every new organization starts **unverified** until an admin reviews it. Volunteers see an **Unverified** chip on its pages: "Hours here won't appear on verified letters until this organization is verified." While unverified:

- Hours served there are left off verified letters.
- Volunteers under 18 can't sign up for its shifts.
- Its coordinators see only display names for volunteers under 18 ("Contact hidden until your organization is verified").

When verification is granted, these limits lift automatically. Changing the organization's name or EIN sets it back to unverified.

## Archiving and deleting

- An organization with volunteer history can be **archived but not deleted**, so past hours stay verifiable.
- Before archiving, cancel upcoming shifts that still have volunteers.
