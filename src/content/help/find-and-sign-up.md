## Find a shift

Open **Explore** to see upcoming shifts. Open one to see the date, time, place, seats left, and the organization. Times always show in the organization's time zone with a label, for example "Sat, Oct 17, 9:00 AM CDT".

If the organization shows an **Unverified** chip, hours you serve there won't appear on verified letters until it is verified.

## Sign up

Press **Sign up**. The button shows that it is working until the server confirms your spot, then it changes to **Signed up**. Nothing is saved until you see that label.

## What the button means

- **Sign up**: a seat is free.
- **Signed up**: you have a spot. You can cancel from here or from My Shifts.
- **Ages N+**: you are younger than this shift's minimum age.
- **Not available yet**: volunteers under 18 can join after this organization is verified.
- **Full**: every seat is taken.
- **Shift started**: you can't sign up once a shift begins.
- **Cancelled by organization**: the organization cancelled this shift.

Waitlists are coming soon. When they arrive, a full shift will offer Join waitlist.

## Cancelling

You can cancel any time before the shift starts. Two things to know:

- Cancelling less than 24 hours before the start is recorded as a late cancel.
- A cancelled signup is final for that shift. If plans change again, pick another date.
