Found a shift you might do later, or an organization you want to keep an eye on? Save it.

## Save something

- On a shift page, press **Save**.
- On an organization page, press **Save**. This is handy when the organization has "No upcoming shifts right now."
- On **Explore**, you can save shifts from the list.

Saving does not sign you up and does not hold a seat. To get a spot, press **Sign up** on the shift.

## Find your saved items

1. Open the avatar menu in the header.
2. Choose **Saved**.

The Saved page lists **saved shifts** and **saved organizations**. Press an item to open it.

## Remove an item

On the Saved page, press **Remove** next to the item.

## Privacy

Your saved list is private. Organizations can't see who saved them, and saving is never shown on public pages.
