**Explore** helps you find a shift worth doing. It has three parts: shifts picked for you, a search box with filters, and organizations.

## Recommended for you

The **Recommended** row shows shifts that match the interests, skills, availability, and ZIP code in your profile. Each one has a short line saying why it was picked.

If you see "Add interests to see shifts picked for you.", press **Add interests** and choose the causes you care about. Recommendations use only your own profile, on your device, and never your attendance record.

## Search

Type words like "food bank", "tutoring", or an organization's name. Small typos are fine.

## Filters

- **Cause area**, such as hunger, animals, or education.
- **Date range**, and **day and time**, such as weekend mornings.
- **Type**: one-time, recurring, virtual, or skilled.
- **Distance** from you. This needs a ZIP code in your profile.
- **Seats available**: hide full shifts.
- **Eligible for my age**: hide shifts with a higher minimum age.
- **Verified organizations only**: useful if you need hours for a verified letter.

If nothing matches, you'll see "No shifts match these filters." Press **Clear filters** to start over.
